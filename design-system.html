Design system

Everything on this site is built from the tokens in css/tokens.css. This page reads them live from the stylesheet, so it always shows what the site actually uses. Switch the theme with the half-moon at the top right to see both palettes.

Color

Semantic tokens. Components use these, never raw hex values.

  • --color-bg
  • --color-surface
  • --color-text
  • --color-text-muted
  • --color-accent
  • --color-media-bg

Type

Faculty Glyphic only for the site name and the home and About intro statements. Hanken Grotesk (variable) for everything else. Space Mono for captions. Sizes are fluid between a 375px phone and a 1920px desktop.

Space

One scale for every gap. Larger steps are fluid.

  • --space-2xs
  • --space-xs
  • --space-s
  • --space-m
  • --space-l
  • --space-xl
  • --space-2xl
  • --space-3xl

Motion

Deliberately quiet: content fades up a few pixels as it arrives, and nothing bounces. The light and dark themes crossfade. Everything stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

Words ease up into place as the page loads.

  • --dur-fast
  • --dur-base
  • --dur-slow
  • --dur-theme
  • --ease-out

Accessibility

  • Semantic landmarks, one h1 per page, and a skip link.
  • Text and accent colors meet 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.
  • Visible focus rings; keyboard access to the menu, slider, lightbox, and form.
  • The mobile menu makes the page inert while open; dialogs trap focus.
  • The slideshow pauses on hover and focus and stops once a visitor steps through it; reduced-motion preferences are respected everywhere.
  • The floating emoji are decoration only and hidden from screen readers.
