Design system

Everything on this site is built from the tokens in css/tokens.css. This page reads them live from the stylesheet, so it always shows what the site actually uses. Switch the theme in the sidebar to see both palettes.

Color

Semantic tokens. Components use these, never raw hex values.

  • --color-bg
  • --color-surface
  • --color-text
  • --color-text-muted
  • --color-accent
  • --color-media-bg

Type

Faculty Glyphic for titles and navigation. Hanken Grotesk (variable) for body copy. Space Mono for captions. Sizes are fluid between a 375px phone and a 1920px desktop.

Motion

Text bumps in word by word with a small overshoot (--ease-spring). Everything stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

Words rise into place, one after another, with a little bounce at the end.

  • --dur-fast
  • --dur-base
  • --dur-slow
  • --ease-out
  • --ease-spring

Accessibility

  • Semantic landmarks, one h1 per page, and a skip link.
  • Text and accent colors meet 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.
  • Visible focus rings; keyboard access to the menu, slider, lightbox, and form.
  • The mobile menu makes the page inert while open; dialogs trap focus.
  • The slideshow can be paused; reduced-motion preferences are respected everywhere.
  • The floating emoji are decoration only and hidden from screen readers.
